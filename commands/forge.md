description: Idea forge — recombine your own inventory (warm channels, live assets, reusable stack) into distribution-shaped venture candidates, pre-scored against your settled kills
---

Proactive ideation from YOUR OWN inventory — the opposite of reactive `/triage`. Most idea-screening tooling is all KILL-side (a go/no-go process, a triage filter, a kill registry): nothing recombines what you already have into ideas shaped to survive your own filters in the first place. This command is that missing generative half.

Protocol:

1. **Load the kill context FIRST — don't re-derive settled verdicts.** Read `<YOUR_KILL_LOG>` (wherever you record previously-killed ideas and why), any standing "verdict" memories, and whatever files hold past viability-research findings and decisions.

2. **Load the inventory:** a reusable-solutions registry (whatever you've already built that could be repurposed — reduces build cost for any new idea), your live pipeline of warm channels (people, not markets — who already trusts you enough to answer a message), any live traffic/usage signals from things you already run, and your own list of active projects.

3. **Generate 5–8 recombinations** of inventory × warm channel × live signal. HARD FILTERS — auto-reject, stating which filter fired:
   - **Distribution shape must be publish / inbound / proxy.** If your growth channel depends on cold outreach at scale, that's usually a losing shape — publish-shaped ideas (something people find and share) survive; DM-shaped ones tend to die unsent.
   - **Must clear the settled kills.** If it pattern-matches a killed idea, say so and stop unless something MATERIAL changed since the kill (state what).
   - **Screen incumbents first.** "Nobody serves X" is usually false — assume an incumbent exists until verified otherwise.
   - **A pivot is a new idea — re-screen it.** If the idea changes shape mid-run (marketplace → tool, SaaS → content, product → service), every filter re-fires on the NEW shape. An incumbent screen run on the original idea proves nothing about the replacement — screen again before building, never after.
   - **Distribution is usually the binding constraint** — an idea that adds supply without a distribution edge is dead on arrival.

4. **Score survivors** (warm-channel strength · build cost GIVEN registry reuse · distribution shape · genuine differentiation). Present at most 3, ranked, each with the ONE cheap validation step (a demand test, never a build).

5. **Close the loop:** log all candidates (including rejects, with the filter that killed them) to your kill/ideas log with the date. Anything you like → run it through `/triage`, then your full viability-proofing process. Never auto-build; the forge produces candidates, the ladder decides.

`<YOUR_KILL_LOG>` — point this at wherever you keep a running list of killed ideas, e.g. a markdown file at your projects root (see `/triage` in this repo, which reads the same file).
